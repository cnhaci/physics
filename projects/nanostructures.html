Light–Matter Interactions in Nanostructures

A computational study of light–matter interactions in a nanoscale system, focusing on resonance behaviour and surface charge distribution.

Project Overview

This project investigates the interaction between light and a nanostructured system through numerical simulations. The study focuses on resonance behaviour, surface charge distribution, and the simulation of a gold nanoparticle sphere.

Project Focus

Resonance

Investigation of the surface charge distribution at a resonance wavelength of 530 nm.

Surface Charge

Numerical visualization of the surface charge distribution during resonance.

Gold Nanoparticle

Simulation of a spherical gold nanoparticle and its numerical representation.

Numerical Model

The nanoparticle sphere is represented using a triangular discretization consisting of 796 triangles.

Results

The simulations illustrate the surface charge distribution at resonance and the numerical representation of a gold nanoparticle sphere.

The simulated sphere consists of 796 triangles, using the relation 2n − 4 with n = 400.
